# Muscle atlas: where each electrode is

> User Manual → Chapter "Muscle exam" → replaces the section "Muscle atlas and electrode placement".

The body drawing has been redone: a whole body in medical-illustration line
style, seen from the **front**, the **back** and the **side**, plus zoomed
**areas** (face, neck, chest and abdomen, back, upper arm, forearm and hand,
hand, leg and foot — each with a right and a left side). Choose the view or
the area in the *View* box. In Simple, the same drawing appears as "Where the
electrodes are (body drawing)".

**Placing an electrode.** Click *Add electrode* and then on the body. The
point **snaps to the recommended spot** for the nearest muscle (the muscle
belly, according to the SENIAM reference; on the face: masseter, temporalis,
frontalis, orbicularis oculi and zygomaticus) or stays where you clicked.
Each electrode is a numbered point; drag to move it, double-click renames
it, *Delete* removes it. An electrode placed in a zoomed area also appears on
the whole body.

**Seeing the activation.** During the exam, the muscle intensity appears **at
the electrode point**, as a heat spot that grows and gets hotter (blue →
yellow → red), clipped by the body outline. The ring around the number shows
the signal quality.

Montages made in earlier versions remain valid: the electrodes are converted
to the new drawing without losing the muscle, the channel or the name.
