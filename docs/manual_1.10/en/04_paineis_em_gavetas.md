# Panels as drawers (Complete level)

> User Manual → Chapter "Complete level (research)" → new section after "The tabs".

In the Complete level, every panel in the **Muscles**, **Heart**, **Eyes**,
**Analyses**, **Filters and Channels** and **Network and Events** tabs has
become a *drawer*: a frame with a thin header holding the panel name and
three controls:

- **▾ / ▸** collapses or expands the panel (clicking the title also works);
- **⋯** opens the panel menu: *Move up*, *Move down*, *Default size* and
  *Close*;
- **×** closes the panel (it disappears from the tab; reopen it with the
  *Panels* button).

On the bottom edge of each drawer there is a **handle**: drag it to change
the panel height; double-click returns to the default size. Side-by-side
panels (the heart trace and the tachogram; the FFT and the bands) share the
width through a divider that you can also drag.

The **Panels ▾** button, at the top of the tab, lists its panels (tick to
show, untick to hide) and offers *Collapse all*, *Expand all* and *Restore
default*.

The arrangement (order, open/collapsed/closed, heights and dividers) is
saved on its own and comes back the same the next time. A closed or
collapsed panel uses no processing: its heavy calculations (fatigue
spectrogram, non-linear HRV, oculometry…) stay stopped until you reopen it;
the session counters keep counting.

Some panels are the core of the tab (the muscle channels, the heart and eye
traces, the exam mode) and therefore have no close button. In the Simple
level none of this appears: the exam screen stays as it was.
