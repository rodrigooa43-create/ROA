# Only the channels in use appear

> User Manual → Chapter "Muscle exam" → note in the section "Muscle activity (channels × time)"; and Chapter "Filters and channels" → note in "Number of channels".

The channel lists and the maps that show one channel per row (muscle
activity over time, muscle spectrogram channel, fatigue channel, heart
channel, horizontal and vertical eye channels, ERP and Custom Layout
channels, impedance table) show **only the channels that are in use** on the
device — 8, 16, 32 or 64 — and only those of the right type (for example,
only the channels marked as muscle). Before, with 8 muscle channels, the
activity map listed CH1 through CH64.

When you change the number of channels (in *Connection* or on the home
screen) or the type of a channel (in *Filters and Channels*), these lists
rebuild themselves and keep the channel that was selected, if it is still in
use. In *Review a recording*, the lists follow the channels of the open
recording, not those of the device.
